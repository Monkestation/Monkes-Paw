fish_mount
/obj/structure/fish_mount

A wallmounted structure on which a fish can be attached to be used as room decoration.

Vars

mounted_fishThe instance of the fish mounted on this.
persistence_idThe identifier for mounts that carry the trophy between rounds.
persistence_loaded_fishTrophies from persistence have a good chance to be dusted if removal is attempted, though rarely it pays off.

Procs

add_first_fishAdds a random fish to the mount, with a fluff name of who caught it
add_fishMounts the fish on the trophy mount
get_fish_beautyReturns the beauty of the mount with the fish on it, based on the fish and its material
load_trophy_fishLoads the trophy fish from previous rounds, or adds a random one if there's none
on_fish_attack_handStarts removing the fish when it's clicked with an empty hand
remove_fishRemoves the fish from the mount after a short delay, rolling for its safe removal if it's from a previous round
roll_for_safe_removalRolls for the safe removal of a fish from a previous round, dusting it on failure
